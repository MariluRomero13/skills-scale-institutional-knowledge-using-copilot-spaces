# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central entry point to OctoAcme’s project management process guidance.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme uses an iterative, customer-focused project lifecycle. Projects begin by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and agreeing on success measures. The team then plans scope, backlog, milestones, ownership, risks, and dependencies. During execution, progress is tracked through the project board, regular team and stakeholder communication, testing, and blocker escalation. Releases follow readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, teams capture learnings and track improvement actions. Clear roles and responsibilities support the process throughout.
